Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Peace Media publishing tool · Effective October 1, 2026 (updated October 1, 2026)

In short: this is a private tool we use to publish videos to our own YouTube channels. It uses the YouTube API Services only for those channels. It does not collect information about YouTube viewers, does not access anyone else's channel, and does not sell or share data for advertising.

1. Who we are and what the tool is

Peace Media operates the YouTube channel Peace AI Briefing (@PeaceAIBriefing), which explains new AI research papers and AI industry news, and is preparing a second channel about Canadian housing and mortgage rates. We built an internal tool (the "API Client") that prepares our videos and publishes them to these channels. The tool is used only by our team and is not offered to anyone else.

Videos are produced with AI assistance. Before publication, every video must pass an originality check against our earlier videos, daily videos are fact-checked against their sources, and new formats are approved by a person video by video. Each video's description links its sources and states that AI assistance was used.

2. YouTube API Services

The API Client uses YouTube API Services. By using it, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What information the tool accesses

Only data of the YouTube channels we own, after we sign in and grant access through Google's OAuth consent screen:

ThroughWhatWhy
YouTube Data APIUpload our videos; set their thumbnails; create our playlists and add our videos to them; upload subtitle tracks; read the status and public statistics (views, likes) of our own videosTo publish and organise our videos
YouTube Analytics APIAggregated reports about our own channels: views, watch time, average percentage viewed, audience retention, traffic sources, countries, subscribers gainedTo learn which of our formats viewers find useful

The tool does not access other creators' channels, comments, private messages, or any personal information about individual viewers. YouTube Analytics reports are aggregated and do not identify viewers.

4. What we store

  • For our own videos: video IDs and links, titles, scripts, publishing records, playlist IDs.
  • Aggregated analytics reports about our own channels.
  • OAuth tokens that let the tool act for our channels.

All of this is stored on a computer operated by our team. It is not published or sold.

5. How information is used and shared

  • Information is used only to publish our videos and to improve them.
  • We use Anthropic's Claude API to draft scripts from public sources and to summarise our own channels' aggregated statistics into internal reports. These requests contain our own content and aggregated statistics only, no viewer information. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • We do not share information with any other third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

6. Retention and deletion

This tool accesses only channels we own. Access can be revoked at any time on Google's security settings page, myaccount.google.com/permissions; if we revoke it, we delete the YouTube data stored for that channel.

7. Security

Credentials and OAuth tokens are stored only on the operating computer, are excluded from any shared copies of the code, and are never sent anywhere other than Google's authentication servers.

8. Terms of Use

  • The API Client is an internal tool for publishing to channels owned by Peace Media. It is not available to the public.
  • Anyone using it agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and to follow the YouTube API Services Terms and Developer Policies.
  • Our videos are provided for information and education only and are not financial, legal or investment advice.
